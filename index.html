Nofar Europe – Strategically Transforming PV into Dispatchable Power

1. Strategic Shift: Standalone PV to Co-Located Ecosystems

  • Portfolio-Wide Mandate: Nofar is systematically hybridizing its existing and under-development PV portfolio with Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) to capture high-margin revenue and de-risk generation
  • Connection Efficiency: By co-locating BESS on existing grid connections, we bypass current 5–10 year queue delays and reduce Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS) through shared infrastructure
  • Dispatchability Alpha: Transitioning from “Passive Generators” to “Active Power Plants” allows us to capture higher prices

Current Hybridization Benchmarks

  • 🇪🇸 Spain: Strategic self-development of 1.3 GWh BESS specifically targeted at existing grid connections to mitigate midday solar cannibalization
  • 🇷🇴 Romania: Turning a pure solar portfolio into a flexibility hub with 1.3 GWh of BESS in pre-construction, including the 849 MW PV projects currently connecting
  • 🇬🇧 UK & 🇮🇹 Italy: Leveraging the Noventum, Atlantic Green and Sunprime platforms to deliver firm-power profiles that meet the 24/7 clean energy requirements of AI and Data Centers

Nofar Europe – Transforming PV into Dispatchable Power (cont.)

The Financial Impact

  • Capture Price Protection: Hybridization increases the average “Capture Price” of our assets by up to 20–30% compared to standalone PV peers
  • Revenue Stacking: Assets now participate in high-frequency arbitrage, ancillary services (FCR/aFRR), and the emerging German Inertia Market
  • Natural Hedge: Internalized trading platform (Nexte) eliminates negative-price exposure and maximizes the “Energy Price Bridge”

Nofar Europe – Strategic Advantage – Trading & Margin Capture

From Asset Owner to Powerhouse

  • Nofar has recently acquired a trading platform in Romania, Nexte, and is establishing a new Pan-European trading platform
  • Vertical Integration: Moving from a passive IPP to an active Powerhouse by acquiring Nexte and initiating an in-house trading platform

Market Overview

European energy landscape and BESS opportunity

European Market Landscape (2026 Strategic Outlook)

  • The European market has entered a structural maturation phase. While 2025 saw a slight contraction in pure solar installs, 2026 is the “Year of Flexibility.” The primary value driver has shifted from Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) to Capture Prices.
  • The Problem: Solar cannibalization during peak hours (50–60% capture rates in Spain/Germany)
  • The Nofar Solution: Hybridization and BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) to shift generation to high-price hours
  • The European energy transition has entered an “Operational Phase” where value is migrating from sheer capacity to flexible, dispatchable generation. Nofar Europe is positioned at the intersection of this shift.

🇮🇹 Italy – Market Overview

Market Fundamentals

  • Top European Solar Hub: Installed base of 43.5 GW in 2025. Solar surging 25% YoY to generate 44 TWh in 2025.
  • Ambitious 2030 Targets: 79.2 GW of solar and 28.1 GW of wind by 2030, with renewables covering 63% of electricity consumption.
  • Critical Flexibility Need: As coal is phased out, a 59 GW increase in variable generation requires flexibility.
  • CfD Frameworks: Auction-based frameworks offering 20-year two-way Contracts for Difference.

The BESS Opportunity

  • Massive Scale-Up: Total storage capacity reached ~7.4 GW (17.9 GWh) by 2025.
  • MACSE Framework: EC approved €17.7 billion state aid scheme to procure 9 GW / 71 GWh of centralized storage via long-term capacity contracts through 2033.
  • TIDE Reform: 15-minute market intervals for intraday and imbalance settlement.
  • Southern Synergy: Hybrid solar-plus-storage is the standard in high-penetration regions like Sicily and Puglia.

🇬🇧 UK – Market Overview

Market Fundamentals

  • Renewables-Led Generation: In 2025, renewables became GB’s largest electricity source, providing 47% of total generation. Wind power accounts for over 57% of all renewable output.
  • Coal-Free Grid: GB closed its last coal-fired plant in September 2024.
  • Clean Power 2030 Ambition: The government’s plan targets clean generation exceeding domestic demand by 2030.
  • Grid Connection Reform: NESO overhauled to a “First Ready, First Connected” model. Projects meeting “Gate 2” criteria receive priority.

The BESS Opportunity

  • Accelerated Capacity Build: Flexible energy capacity forecast to increase 26% by 2040. Battery storage projected to grow from 5 GW to 16 GW by 2040.
  • Diversified Revenue Stacking: BESS assets secure returns by “stacking” multiple streams: wholesale energy arbitrage and ancillary services.
  • High-Yield Asset Class: Standalone BESS projects achieving IRRs of ~13.2% for 2029 entry years.
  • Long Duration (LDES) Support: Ofgem’s new Cap and Floor scheme aims to bring up to 7.7 GW online by 2035.

🇩🇪 Germany – Market Overview

Market Fundamentals

  • Largest European Hub: Europe’s largest electricity producer and consumer, with ~432 TWh in 2024 and nearly 100 GW of flexible assets expected by 2030.
  • Renewables Surge: 59% of generation in 2024, rising to 67.5% in Q2 2025. Legally mandated to reach 80% by 2030.
  • Thermal Phase-Out: Total coal exit by 2038 and carbon neutrality by 2045.

The BESS Opportunity

  • Explosive Capacity Growth: Utility-scale battery capacity projected to surge from 2.4 GW in 2025 to 11 GW by 2029, reaching 40 GW by 2060.
  • High-Yield Configurations: 2-hour duration batteries delivering IRRs of ~21% for 2026 entry.
  • New Revenue Stacking: Market-based procurement for inertia launches in Jan 2026, offering fixed premium upside for grid-forming batteries.

🇷🇴 Romania – Market Overview

Market Fundamentals

  • Largest Growth Hub in SEE: Power market capacity of ~25.3 GW in 2025, projected to reach ~38.9 GW by 2031 (7.42% CAGR).
  • Renewables Surge: 68% of the power market share in 2025. Solar capacity crossed 7 GW.
  • Coal Phase-Out: Lignite-fired generation (2.6 GW) slated for complete decommissioning by 2032.
  • Liberalized Pricing: Retail prices fully liberalized in July 2025, driving demand for corporate PPAs.

The BESS Opportunity

  • Breakthrough Capacity: Projected to reach 2.2 GW by end of 2026, up from just 150 MW in early 2025.
  • High-Yield Potential: Standalone BESS projects achieving double-digit IRRs, with hybrid solar-plus-storage showing even higher promise post-2028.
  • Supportive Funding: Government committed €300M+ via EU Modernisation Fund specifically for BESS.
